Quick check

random.nextInt(words.length) with 12 words can give ...

  1. any whole number from 0 to 11
  2. any whole number from 1 to 12
  3. 0 to 12
  4. only even numbers

Pick one — the answer is on the next slide.

Answer

random.nextInt(words.length) with 12 words can give ...

  1. any whole number from 0 to 11
  2. any whole number from 1 to 12
  3. 0 to 12
  4. only even numbers

nextInt(12) never gives 12 - exactly the valid indexes of a 12-element array.
