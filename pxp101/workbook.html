Student homework book

PXP101 · Monster Munch

One chapter per week. Do the challenges on your own project - there is no separate file to make.

Read this first

Nothing here needs the internet except PixelPad itself. The game runs entirely in the browser - there is no server, no account and no API key anywhere in this course. A finished week is a single file that plays on any computer.

The child draws the art. Every sprite is listed with the exact size to draw it. The generated games use plain coloured rectangles as stand-ins so the code can be tested; they are meant to be replaced.

Chapter 1

Your monster appears

A game is made of objects you draw and then tell what to do. Today you draw a monster and write four lines of Python that put it on the screen.

What you learned

  • A sprite is a picture you draw.
  • start happens once, right at the beginning.
  • y is up and down. Minus numbers are down.
  • Monster() makes one monster.

Words to know

spritestartroomx and ymaking an object

Challenges

Challenge 1. Make it yours

Redraw monster.png so it looks like a monster YOU invented. Same size, 48 by 48.

Done when: You press Play and your own drawing is on the screen.

Challenge 2. Move it

Change -210 to a different number and press Play. Try -100. Try 0. Try 200.

Done when: You can say what bigger and smaller numbers do to the monster.

Stuck?

Open week 1's code and compare it with yours line by line. The green lines are the ones added that week.

Chapter 2

It follows your mouse

loop happens over and over, many times every second. That is what makes a game move. Today your monster follows your mouse, and learns to stop at the edges.

What you learned

  • loop happens over and over, about sixty times a second.
  • mouse_x() is where your mouse is, left to right.
  • if means only when.
  • The line under an if is indented four spaces.

Words to know

loopmouse_x()ifthe edges of the screen

Challenges

Challenge 1. Squeeze it in

Change both 320s to 150 and press Play. Then try 400.

Done when: You can explain what the number is measuring.

Challenge 2. Upside down

Add self.y = mouse_y() under the follow line and press Play.

Done when: Your monster follows the mouse everywhere, and you can say why.

Stuck?

Open week 2's code and compare it with yours line by line. The green lines are the ones added that week.

Chapter 3

Food to catch

A game needs something to DO. Today you draw food and make a new KIND of thing - a Food - that shows up high on the screen at a random spot every time you press Play.

What you learned

  • A class is a kind of thing. Food is our second one.
  • import random gives you surprise numbers.
  • random.randint(-300, 300) is a random number between two numbers.
  • Food() makes one piece of food, just like Monster() makes a monster.

Words to know

a second classimport randomrandom.randint()

Challenges

Challenge 1. Draw a better snack

Redraw food.png as something you would really want to catch. Same size, 30 by 30.

Done when: You press Play and your own food is on the screen.

Challenge 2. Change the spread

Change the -300 and 300 to -100 and 100 and press Play a few times. Then try -400 and 400.

Done when: You can say what those two numbers change about where the food lands.

Stuck?

Open week 3's code and compare it with yours line by line. The green lines are the ones added that week.

Chapter 4

It falls

loop runs about sixty times a second. If you take a little off y every single loop, the food slides down the screen. And when it drops off the bottom, we send it back up to fall again - for ever.

What you learned

  • Game.fallSpeed is a number the whole game shares.
  • Taking a little off y every loop makes something fall.
  • if means only when.
  • When the food drops off the bottom, we recycle it - back to the top to fall again.

Words to know

a number you can changefalling by changing yrecycling an object

Challenges

Challenge 1. Faster, slower

Change Game.fallSpeed = 3 to 1, press Play, then to 8. Watch the difference.

Done when: You can say what fallSpeed does to the game.

Challenge 2. Change the bottom

Change the -260 in the recycle if to -100 and press Play. Where does the food jump back now?

Done when: You can explain what the -260 is measuring.

Stuck?

Chapter 5

Catch it

get_collision asks: are these two things touching? When the monster touches the food, we add one to the score and send the food below the floor - so last week's recycle lifts it straight back to the top.

What you learned

  • Your score and your lives are numbers the whole game shares.
  • get_collision(self, 'Monster') is True when two things are touching.
  • Game.score = Game.score + 1 means take the score and make it one bigger.
  • Sending the food to -300 lets last week's recycle lift it back up.

Words to know

a score you keepget_collision()changing a number by adding

Challenges

Challenge 1. Worth more

Change the + 1 in the catch to + 5 and press Play. Catch some food.

Done when: You can say what the number after the plus controls.

Challenge 2. Predict it

Before you press Play, tell someone what will happen if you change Game.score = 0 to Game.score = 100.

Done when: You made a guess first, then checked it.
