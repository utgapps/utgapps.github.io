  1. The middle of the screen

  4. The top right

0, 0 is the middle; plus x is right and plus y is up.

Why does the mouse need a Cursor object? (this week)

  1. To draw the mouse
  2. To make the mouse faster
  3. So a card can ask whether it is touching it
  4. Python needs one

Touching is between two objects - the cursor stands in for the mouse.
