15-week course

PY302 · Strategy Game

Fifteen weeks building a lane-battle strategy game in Python. Send slimes down three lanes, hold off bats and golems, and knock down the enemy's base before it knocks down yours. You write every line.

What you are building

A lane-battle strategy game. Click a card to send a slime down its lane; the enemy base sends bats, and now and then a golem, back. Units that meet fight, units that reach a base strike it, and health bars show who is winning. Your army has a cap, a mana bar shows what is left, a start screen picks Easy or Hard, and a ghost walks straight through the battle.

The book Teacher curriculum Homework book Open PixelPad

The book is the one to hand a child: every step, every line, chapter by chapter. Each week page carries that week’s slides and links straight to its lesson plan.

Before you start — read this

Nothing here needs the internet except the code editor itself. The game runs entirely in the browser - there is no server, no account and no API key anywhere in this course.

The students draw the art. Every sprite is listed with the exact size to draw it. The generated games use plain coloured rectangles as stand-ins so the code can be tested; they are meant to be replaced. The slime, the bat, the golem and the ghost are sprite sheets - eight frames in one picture - and week 3 explains how to draw one.

This game is played with the mouse, and one key. Click into the game before you click a card. After a win or a loss, the space bar starts another round.

The weeks

Week 1

The battlefield

A strategy game starts with its map. Today you build the field - a background, three bases of yours, three bridges and the enemy's base - and a for loop places the three lanes so you only type them once.

Week 2

Cards and a cursor

You play this game by clicking cards. A card is just an object with a picture - and to know when the mouse is on one, you give the mouse an object of its own that follows it everywhere.

Week 3

A slime from a card

Click a card and a slime appears. The card asks two questions every loop - is the cursor touching me, and was the button just pressed - and only when both are yes does it make a unit.

Week 4

Down the lanes

A slime marches by its speed, and the outer lanes bend in towards the enemy. min() and max() pick the smaller and the bigger of two numbers - which is all a bend in the road needs.

Week 5

The enemy sends its own

The enemy base runs a timer: every second it makes a unit of its own. Every unit knows its team, and an enemy is given a minus speed - a minus number walks the other way.

Week 6

Bats, and a battle

The enemy sends bats down a random lane, flipped to face you. When units of two teams touch, each takes the other's attack off its health - get_collision_list() finds everything you touch at once, and a for loop deals with each one.

Week 7

Win or lose

A unit that reaches a base on the other team strikes it and is spent. A base keeps its own health, and the moment one falls the game changes room - to YouWin or to YouLose.

Week 8

Health bars, and another round

A base shows its health as a bar that shrinks. The bar is an object of its own that the base keeps under a name, moves to itself every loop, and squeezes with scale - health divided by the most it ever had.

Week 9

Only so many

Strategy needs a limit. The game counts how many units you have out in game. - every card adds its cost, every unit that dies gives it back - and a card only works while there is room.

Week 10

Easy or hard

A game starts with a menu. A Menu room shows two buttons, and the one you click sets how big your army can be before it sends you to Play.

Week 11

The golem

One enemy in ten is a golem: twice the size, ten times the health, and half the speed. A second random roll, inside the spawn, turns a bat into a golem after it is made.

Week 12

A bar for the golem

Every unit gets a health bar, hidden - and the golem shows its own. A unit that owns an object must take it away when it goes, or the bar is left floating where it died.

Week 13

The ghost card

A fourth card makes a ghost. It is the same Card class with four values changed - its lane, its cost, its picture and one boolean that says it is a ghost - and the card passes the difference on to the unit it makes.

Week 14

Ghost powers

A ghost walks through the battle: nothing hits it and it hits nothing, until it reaches the base. not turns a True into a False - and the golem's bar stops flashing once it is put in place the moment it is made.

Week 15

Mana, and a stronger enemy

A mana bar shows how much of your army is left to spend, and the enemy sends its units a little faster every time - max() keeps it from getting impossibly fast.
