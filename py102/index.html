15-week course

PY102 · Platformer

Fifteen weeks building a platformer in Python. Pixelhead runs, falls, jumps and lands; wasps patrol the sky; portals lead to the next level. You write every line - gravity included.

What you are building

A two-level platformer. Run with the arrow keys, jump with space, stomp wasps from above and dodge them from below, and step through the portals to win - with three hearts, and a fresh start when you lose them.

The book Teacher curriculum Homework book Open PixelPad

The book is the one to hand a child: every step, every line, chapter by chapter. Each week page carries that week’s slides and links straight to its lesson plan.

Before you start — read this

Nothing here needs the internet except the code editor itself. The game runs entirely in the browser - there is no server, no account and no API key anywhere in this course.

The students draw the art. Every sprite is listed with the exact size to draw it. The generated games use plain coloured rectangles as stand-ins so the code can be tested; they are meant to be replaced.

The weeks

Week 1

Meet Pixelhead

Every game is built from objects. Today you make a Player class, give it a sprite, and put Pixelhead into your first room, Level1.

Week 2

Wasps in the sky

Every spot on the screen has an address. Today you learn the x and y grid, make a FlyEnemy class, and use the dot to put two wasps exactly where you want them.

Week 3

Things that move

Start runs once; loop runs forever. Today the wasps fly with loop, and Pixelhead walks left and right while you hold the arrow keys.

Week 4

A world to stand in

Pixelhead should face the way it walks, and stand on something. Today a minus scaleX flips the picture, a background goes up behind everything, and grass tiles make the first floor.

Week 5

Wasps on patrol

The wasps fly away and never come back. Today they shrink to size, and two ifs turn them around at each side of the screen - facing the way they fly.

Week 6

Gravity and the ground

In the real world things fall. Today Pixelhead falls all the time - and get_collision tells it when it is touching the floor, so it can stop.

Week 7

Jump!

To reach high ground you have to jump. Today space throws Pixelhead upward with key_was_pressed, and two more tiles make a platform worth jumping to.

Week 8

A smooth jump

A real jump rises over time. Today a jump timer counts down: while it is above zero Pixelhead rises, and space only sets it going.

Week 9

Only from the ground

Pixelhead can still jump in mid-air. Today a boolean - True or False - remembers whether it is on the ground, else sets it back, and and makes the jump check both.

Week 10

Stomp the wasps

A wasp is dangerous from below and beaten from above. Today Pixelhead asks which wasp it touched, and an if inside an if decides who is destroyed.

Week 11

Bounce, and land on top

What lifts Pixelhead is the jump timer, not the space bar. Today a stomp sets the timer for a bounce, and the floor check learns to look down - so you land ON a platform, not inside it.

Week 12

A portal to Level 2

A game can have more than one level. Today you build a second room, and a Portal that sends Pixelhead there the moment they touch.

Week 13

Where does this portal go?

Every portal goes to Level2 - even the one in Level2. Today each portal gets its own destination, a variable the room sets, and the last one leads to a Win room.

Week 14

Three lives

One sting should not end everything. Today the game itself keeps three lives, a sting costs one, falling off costs them all - and Game loop starts Level1 again when they run out.

Week 15

Hearts on the screen

You cannot see your lives yet. Today three Heart objects, made by the Game, survive every room change because they are persistent, and each one hides when the lives drop below its number.
