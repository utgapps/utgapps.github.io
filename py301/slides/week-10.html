Answer

Why does 'Your final score is: ' + str(game.score) need str()?

  1. To round it
  2. + can not join words and a number
  3. To make it red
  4. It does not

Both sides of + must be words to join them.

Why is previousX kept at the very top of Slicer loop? (this week)

  1. Python needs it first

  4. To make the slicer faster
