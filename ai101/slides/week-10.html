Week 10

Why streaming

Which is better?

  • A: nothing for 5 seconds, then everything
  • B: starts in 0.5s, finishes in 6
  • Most people say B. B is slower.

Fast vs feels fast

  • The model writes one word at a time either way
  • Non-streaming holds them all back
  • Streaming hands them over as they appear

One word breaks everything

stream: true

  • res.json() stops working
  • The body is no longer ONE object
  • Good. Now look at what it actually is.
