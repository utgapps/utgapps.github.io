temperature 0 vs 1.4

What temperature really is

The model ranks every possible next word

  • 0 - always take the top one
  • 1.4 - often take a lower one
  • That is genuinely all it is

fast vs smart

  • fast - about a second, a bit simple
  • smart - slower, thinks better
  • Both share one graphics card
