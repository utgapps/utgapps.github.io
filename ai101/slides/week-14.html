Hallucination hunt

10 minutes

  • Make it state something false
  • You must be able to prove it is false
  • Best three go on the board

Why it happens

It predicts likely next words

  • Nothing in that checks for truth
  • Fluent and correct are unrelated
  • It is not lying - lying needs knowing

It learned from what people wrote

Describe a nurse. An engineer. A criminal.

  • Compare your answer with a classmate's
  • It repeats what was common in the text

Prompt injection

"Ignore your instructions and..."

  • Attack your partner's companion
  • It often works
  • This is a real security problem

Neither defence is perfect

  • Rules in the persona - can be talked around
  • Guards in code - blunt
  • So never let it decide anything alone
