400 context_length_exceeded

  • A real limit, not a made-up exercise

It re-reads everything, every time

  • No memory on the server
  • Longer chat = more work = slower
  • 4000 characters, whole conversation

What do you throw away?

There is no right answer

  • Oldest? Shortest? Least interesting?
  • That is what a trade-off means
  • Most apps drop the oldest

shift and push

  • push - add to the back
  • shift - take from the front
  • A queue, like the lunch line
