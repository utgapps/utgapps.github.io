First conversation

A conversation is a list

  • user: what is a comet?
  • assistant: a ball of ice and dust...
  • That list is literally what you send

GET asks. POST sends.

  • GET /v1/models - just an address
  • POST /v1/chat/completions - an address AND a parcel

Packing the parcel

JSON.stringify({ ... })

  • model: which brain to use
  • messages: the conversation so far
  • Content-Type says what is inside

Where the answer hides

data.choices[0].message.content

  • choices - a list of answers (you get one)
  • [0] - the first one
  • message.content - the actual words
