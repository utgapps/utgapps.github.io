PY202 · Balloon Fight

Fifteen weeks building a physics game in Python. Pixelhead floats under three balloons, pops bubbles for a boost and falls under gravity, while viruses patrol the sky. You write every line.

What you are building

A two-level balloon fight. Steer with the arrow keys by building up speed, pop bubbles to float up, and land on viruses from above - touch one from below and a balloon pops and you fall faster. Clear both levels to win; fall off the bottom and it is game over.

The book Teacher curriculum Homework book Open PixelPad

The book is the one to hand a child: every step, every line, chapter by chapter. Each week page carries that week’s slides and links straight to its lesson plan.

Before you start — read this

Nothing here needs the internet except the code editor itself. The game runs entirely in the browser - there is no server, no account and no API key anywhere in this course.

The students draw the art. Every sprite is listed with the exact size to draw it. The generated games use plain coloured rectangles as stand-ins so the code can be tested; they are meant to be replaced. Week 15 also uses a sound called jump.mp3; until a student uploads one, the editor plays a short beep in its place.

Keep the console open. From week 3 the game writes your speed, the enemies left and the game over message to the console under the game. If a student cannot see it, that is the first thing to fix.

The weeks

Week 1

Pixelhead, a virus and two bubbles

Every game is built from objects. Today you make three classes - Pixelhead, a virus and a bubble - and put one of each kind in a room called Level1.

Week 2

Mountains, and a drift

Start runs once; loop runs forever. Today the sky gets its mountains, the bubbles start to rise, and Pixelhead drifts at a speed kept in a variable.

Week 3

Steer

Pixelhead does not walk - it builds up speed. Today each tap of an arrow changes the speed by one, Pixelhead turns to face the way you tapped, and the console tells you the speed.

Week 4

Up and down

Today a virus patrols up and down. It remembers which way it is going in a variable that holds words, and changes it when it reaches the top or the bottom - using &lt; and &gt;.

Week 5

Side to side

Today you build the sideways patrol yourself from the pattern you made last week - a direction, a move for each one, and a turn at each edge - and the virus faces the way it flies.

Week 6

Three balloons

Pixelhead gets three balloons. Pixelhead makes them in its own start, keeps a name for each, and moves them to sit above its head every loop - one object steering three others with the dot.

Week 7

Stomp or pop

Today things touch. Land on a virus from above and it is gone; touch one from below and Pixelhead is gone - an if inside an if, with else for the other way. And bubbles pop.

Week 8

A bubble machine

Two bubbles are not a game. Today an invisible Spawner makes a new bubble every two seconds with a timer, and each one starts somewhere random along the bottom.

Week 9

Level 2

Clear the sky and you go to level 2. To know when the sky is clear, the game counts the viruses - in the Game class, the one object that lasts through every room.

Week 10

Gravity, and a boost

Today Pixelhead falls. gravity pulls it down a little every loop, and popping a bubble starts a timer that lifts it up for two-thirds of a second.

Week 11

One balloon at a time

Losing everything at the first touch is harsh. Today a virus pops one balloon at a time, the game counts how many are left, and elif picks which one to pop.

Week 12

Invincible

After a hit, Pixelhead gets a moment of safety: a timer that must run out before the next virus counts. Then the viruses learn to place themselves, so level 2 can have some.

Week 13

New level, same balloons

Lose a balloon in level 1 and level 2 hands it back - and it never follows you. Today the balloons a level builds come from the count, and every lost balloon makes Pixelhead heavier.

Week 14

Game over

A game needs an ending. Today losing your last balloon or falling off the bottom goes to a game over room, and space starts again - after putting back every number the last game changed.

Week 15

Sound, a flash and a win

The last week makes it feel finished: a sound when you pop a bubble, Pixelhead flashing while it is safe, and a win screen when level 2 is clear.
