Answer

Which two things end the game?

  1. Pressing space, or a bubble
  2. A stomp, or a bubble
  3. The last balloon pops, or you fall off the bottom
  4. Level 2, or the win screen

Both go to set_room('GameOver').

How long is key_is_pressed True?

  1. As long as the key is held
  2. One loop
  3. Never
  4. Until the next room

It is True for every loop the key is down.
