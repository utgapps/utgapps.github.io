15-week course

PY201 · RPG

Fifteen weeks building a role-playing game in Python. Pixelhead explores a forest, a field and a swamp, collects ore, buys a sword from a merchant and fights wasps that drop random loot. You write every line.

What you are building

A three-room adventure. Walk with the arrow keys, collect ore for money, trade with the merchant with E, swing your sword with F the way you face, and heal in the swamp - with health, wasps that take three hits, and loot that is never the same twice.

The book Teacher curriculum Homework book Open PixelPad

The book is the one to hand a child: every step, every line, chapter by chapter. Each week page carries that week’s slides and links straight to its lesson plan.

Before you start — read this

Nothing here needs the internet except the code editor itself. The game runs entirely in the browser - there is no server, no account and no API key anywhere in this course.

The students draw the art. Every sprite is listed with the exact size to draw it. The generated games use plain coloured rectangles as stand-ins so the code can be tested; they are meant to be replaced.

Keep the console open. From week 3 the game writes money, health and the merchant's words to the console under the game. If a student cannot see it, that is the first thing to fix.

The weeks

Week 1

Into the forest

Every game is built from objects. Today you make Pixelhead and two ores, put the ores in a room called Forest - and find out why Pixelhead has to be persistent to go there too.

Week 2

Four directions

Start runs once; loop runs forever. Today Pixelhead walks in all four directions while you hold the arrows - and changes picture to face the way it walks.

Week 3

Collect the ore

Today the forest gets its picture, Pixelhead learns to stay in front of it, and walking into an ore picks it up - with your money counted in the console.

Week 4

Wasps

The forest gets dangerous. Today you make a FlyEnemy class whose wasps fly across the forest - and a sting removes Pixelhead from the game.

Week 5

Patrols and a merchant

Today the wasps turn round at the edges and patrol, and a merchant moves into the forest who speaks when you press E - using and to ask two questions at once.

Week 6

A sword

The merchant keeps a promise. Today Pixelhead gets a boolean that says whether it has a sword, the merchant switches it on, and F swings it.

Week 7

Which way you face

Pixelhead should swing the way it faces. Today a string remembers the last direction you walked, and angle turns the slash to point that way.

Week 8

Swing and fade

Today the slash appears in FRONT of Pixelhead instead of on top of it, and a timer makes each one disappear half a second after you swing.

Week 9

Fight back

A short week to catch up. Today the wasps notice the sword - a slash destroys the wasp it touches - and a third wasp joins the forest.

Week 10

Health

One sting should not end everything. Today Pixelhead gets three health, a sting costs one, a second timer stops one wasp taking all three at once - and at 0 the forest starts again.

Week 11

Tougher wasps

Today a sting pushes the wasp away, and the wasps get health of their own: three hits to defeat, each slash counting once.

Week 12

Random loot

Today Python picks numbers for you. import brings in the random toolbox, every ore picks its own colour, and a defeated wasp drops loot - half the time.

Week 13

The field

Today the game gets bigger than one screen. Walk off the right edge of the forest and you are in a field; walk off its left edge and you are back. game. lets a room reach Pixelhead.

Week 14

The swamp

Today you build the third room on your own pattern: walk off the bottom of the forest into a swamp, stretch its half-height picture to fill the screen with scaleY, and walk back up.

Week 15

Trade and heal

Today the money means something. The merchant charges three for the sword and tells you when you cannot pay - with else - and a healer in the swamp gives your health back.
